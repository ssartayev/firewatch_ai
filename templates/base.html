SENSE AI is a working demonstration, not a finished product. The building, the people and the sensors in it are simulated. It is not an approved fire-safety system, it has no safety certification, and it cannot promise that a fire will be detected or that everyone will get out. Every people-count you see is an estimate with a stated confidence, never an exact count. The escape routes are a simulation on a simplified map of a building. The camera model is experimental, and any accuracy figure quoted comes from the people who published that model, not from us.
