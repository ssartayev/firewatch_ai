Escape routes

The shortest route is not always the fastest.

If everyone heads for their nearest staircase, one of them jams and the other two stand half empty — and the whole building takes longer to clear. SENSE AI works out how long each route will really take once it is crowded, then splits people between all three. Press a button below to break something and watch the routes move.
