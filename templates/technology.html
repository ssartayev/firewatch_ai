How it works

Sensors are replaceable. The model in the middle is the product.

Every detection model in SENSE AI is a module behind an interface: it returns an estimate and a confidence, and nothing downstream knows or cares what produced it. A better camera model, a different radar vendor or a building's existing fire panel all plug into the same place. What does not get swapped out is the layer that decides how much to trust each of them and what to do about the people the result implies.

How information flows through the system

What the system does, over and over

StepWhat happens
SENSERead every device in the twin: cameras, presence sensors, smoke and heat detectors. Advance the hazard state.
ESTIMATEFuse the layers per space by precision weighting. Publish a range wherever combined precision is low.
PLANAllocate the whole population across every usable exit in parcels, each one seeing the congestion the last one created.
GUIDEDerive per-zone instructions from the allocation. Different zones get different messages because they are going to different cores.
OBSERVEWatch where people actually go. A share of them will not follow the instruction, and that share is measured rather than assumed away.
COMPAREMeasure the gap between planned and observed distribution across the cores.
RECALCULATEReplan when the divergence, the hazard or the load says so. The plan is a snapshot, never a commitment.

What this cannot do — read this part

It is not certified and has no regulatory approval. Nothing here has been assessed against any life-safety standard, and it must not be relied on as, or in place of, a compliant fire alarm and evacuation system.
It does not guarantee detection. The detection models are experimental. Published figures are the model authors' self-reported validation results on their own datasets, not measurements on this building and not evidence of real-world fire performance.
It does not see through walls. The non-visual layer detects motion and micro-motion signatures. Its accuracy depends on mounting, on partition materials and on commissioning, it merges people who stand close together, and it is never treated as an exact count.
It does not command anyone. The responder screen is decision support. A commander decides; the system states its belief, its confidence and its reasoning.
It does not claim novel routing. Dynamic hazard detection, digital building models, changing evacuation routes and dynamic exit signage all exist today. The integration is what is being proposed: confidence-aware occupancy, crowd-flow observation, capacity modelling, population-level allocation and repeated replanning against observed behaviour.
It is not production-ready. This is a simulation on a schematic building, built to evaluate the concept.
