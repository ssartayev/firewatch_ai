Building & sensors

Every sensor knows which room it is in.

The system holds a model of the building: every room, corridor, staircase and door, with how wide each one is and therefore how many people can get through it per minute. Every camera and sensor is tied to a room in that model. Click any room to see what is in it.

Working in this demo

Cameras that count people, radar sensors that see through smoke, smoke and heat detectors, and speakers for announcements. All of them are simulated in software here — but the code that adds their readings up and plans the escape routes is real.

Not built yet

Arrows in the ceiling that light up and point people the right way, and a link into the building's existing fire alarm panel. These are listed above as not built yet. They do nothing in this demo — we are not pretending otherwise.

SENSE AI is a working demonstration, not a finished product. The building, the people and the sensors in it are simulated. It is not an approved fire-safety system, it has no safety certification, and it cannot promise that a fire will be detected or that everyone will get out. Every people-count you see is an estimate with a stated confidence, never an exact count. The escape routes are a simulation on a simplified map of a building. The camera model is experimental, and any accuracy figure quoted comes from the people who published that model, not from us.
